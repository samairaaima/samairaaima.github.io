About

Marketing is just
human connection
with strategy behind it.

I grew up in India, but learned a lot of what I know from the globe: 21 countries, countless airports, and an early understanding that people everywhere want similar things, but respond to very different stories.

I'm a third-year Marketing and Business Analytics student at UBC Sauder. My experience spans across digital marketing, branding, content creation, and community engagement through internships and student organizations. Every role has taught me the same thing: people don't stop for information. They stop for something that feels true.

Outside of marketing, I'm a national-level soccer player, a trained Kathak dancer with a six-year certification, and someone who has probably built half her personality around coffee. None of these sit neatly on a resume under "marketing experience," but they shape how I think: strategy, discipline, and expressing yourself.

I believe the best brands don't describe what they sell. They show you who you become when you use it. That's the work I want to do.

Two brands.

Nothing left out.

Brand Analysis

Spotify Wrapped:

the smartest gift Spotify ever gave you

An analysis of how Spotify Wrapped became more than a year-end recap. It became a global marketing engine built on personalization, shareability, and identity.

The best marketing doesn't feel like marketing at all. It feels like a gift.

They turned cold analytics into something emotional. Your music taste feels tied to your identity, so seeing it reflected back does not feel like a data report. It feels like being seen.

And then something even more powerful happens: people share it. Every person who uploads their Wrapped is doing Spotify's marketing for them, completely voluntarily, to their entire following.

01 / The Overview

Spotify Wrapped is Spotify's annual, data-driven campaign that transforms billions of user listening data points into personalized stories. It reaches 170+ countries every year and turns ordinary analytics into one of the most anticipated cultural moments on the internet.

02 / The Strategic Pillars

Wrapped's success isn't accidental. It's built on four strategic pillars.

◉

Personalization at Scale

Turns data into personal stories that feel uniquely "you."

↑

Shareability by Design

Built for stories. Designed to be posted. Made to go viral.

☻

Identity & Self-Expression

Lets users express who they are through what they listen to.

▦

Anticipation & Ritual

The yearly format builds hype, curiosity, and cultural relevance.

03 / How It Works

From data → insight → story → share.

▤

Collect

Spotify collects listening data all year long.

→
▥

Analyze

Data is grouped into patterns, trends, and milestones.

→
✦

Personalize

Insights are tailored to each user.

→
▦

Visualize

Data is turned into engaging, story-based visuals.

→
↑

Share

Users share their stories, expanding reach organically.

04 / Why It Works

Wrapped hits the sweet spot between data and emotion.

  • Feels personal, but is designed to be public.
  • Turns routine listening into a moment of reflection.
  • Gives users something to proudly share.
  • Creates massive organic reach without heavy ad spend.
"

Wrapped doesn't just show you what you listened to. It shows you who you are.

05 / Impact

More than a campaign. It's a growth engine.

↗

Increased Engagement

Drives app opens, screen time, and user interaction.

♡

Stronger Brand Love

Feels less like marketing, more like a gift.

★

Massive Earned Media

Millions of shares create global awareness for free.

☻

Retention & Loyalty

Deepens emotional connection with the platform.

06 / What Brands Can Learn

Key takeaways for marketers.

  • Make it about the user, not the brand.
  • Turn data into emotion.
  • Design for shareability from the start.
  • Build rituals that people look forward to.
  • Let your audience market for you.
Final Takeaway

Spotify Wrapped is a masterclass in modern marketing. It blends data, design, and culture to create a moment people don't just participate in. They wait for it.

"It's not just a recap.
It's a reflection."
