my take

Business school teaches you frameworks. What it doesn't tell you is that the interesting part is what happens when the framework breaks. These are the projects where I stopped filling in templates and started actually thinking: about brands that mean more than they say, communities that deserve better marketing, and the data that refuses to confirm your hypothesis.
