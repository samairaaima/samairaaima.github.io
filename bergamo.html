Bergamo: A Heavenly Mediterranean Paradise Awaits You

Stanley Communications, New Delhi

My take

A few days before Bergamo's launch, I was handed a brief for a restaurant I had never been to. No visit, no tasting, no walk-through, just a concept and a deadline. What I had to figure out was how to write something that didn't just describe a menu, but made someone feel like they were transported there and not just having another meal. This press release was the result. It taught me that good brand writing isn't about having all the information. It's about finding the right emotion and building everything around it.

The work

Dine in the lap of luxury at Bergamo — an experience that exudes opulence, luxury, and of course, good food.

Housing the magic and beauty of Italy, Bergamo, an exquisite eatery, is situated in the heart of New Delhi, Vasant Kunj. Offering a sense of sophistication and exclusivity, the restaurant is immersed in fine delicacies engulfed in a modern interpretation of Medieval architecture. A palatable paradise, Bergamo is inspired by its namesake Italian city and acts as the ideal location to enjoy a mix of Mediterranean delicacies and authentic European food.

Bergamo is the brainchild of the multifaceted entrepreneur Kunal Chabbra. An alumnus of the British University in Dubai, Kunal is based in the UAE and harbors interests in multiple industries, namely real estate, tourism, and now hospitality.

Bergamo's menu consists of fabulous delicacies developed by Chef Engin ULUC, who has extensive experience in Italian, French, and Turkish cuisines. The chef seeks to provide comfort to patrons through food that is authentic yet reminds one of a meal prepared by one's mother, evoking fond memories of dining with your family. Chef Engin has also taken a keen interest in pairing different wines that would go perfectly with certain dishes from the menu.

The finely curated food menu transports guests to the heart of an enchanting medieval atmosphere. Some of the dishes on the menu include a Spanish starter, patatas bravas, prepared with special spices, French cream sauces carefully prepared with chicken and fish, and an Italian favorite, risotto, cooked with the guest's choice of protein.

The cocktail menu is just as remarkable. Mr. Ulas, the chief mixologist, put together a one-of-a-kind menu meticulously prepared to meet the needs of patrons. With syrups and purées prepared with fresh seasonal fruits, all cocktails are made keeping in mind the Indian flavor palette — less spicy, more sweet and sour, garnished with herbs to match the Mediterranean ambiance.

The ambiance envelops its guests with warmth and comfort. The interior reflects the inherent beauty of the city of Bergamo, renowned for its frescoes and sculptures imitating the shapes of nature. The property's exterior features a magnificent stone wall facade that is rich and bold, instantly leaving you with a feeling of welcome. The entryway boasts a CNC-cut design of the restaurant's mascot, a majestic peacock. A metallic grey textured wall covered with mirrors lends an opulent aura to the space, while the back-lit Corian stone bar counter and tables have a logo engraved in them. The environment created is both functional and memorable.

The property will open its doors at 12 noon and continue till the wee hours of the night, holding up to 150 people.
