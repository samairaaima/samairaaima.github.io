Still Showing Up.

A campaign concept for Rare Beauty about getting ready while figuring life out.

The Strategy
01

The Tension

Beauty shows confidence after the product. Real routines happen before the confidence arrives.

→
02

The Insight

People get ready in moments of uncertainty, anxiety, tiredness, or transition. Makeup becomes a small ritual that helps them show up.

→
03

Our Opportunity

Rare Beauty can own the honest "getting ready while figuring life out" moment, turning everyday routines into stories of self-support.

→
04

Big Idea

Still Showing Up celebrates the tiny choice to keep going, even when you don't feel fully ready.

→
05

Positioning Line

You don't have to have it all together to show up.

The Campaign in Action
Campaign Manifesto

Some days, confidence doesn't come first. Sometimes you get ready while you're still tired. Still nervous. Still unsure. Still figuring it out.

But you put on the blush. You answer the email. You walk into class. You take the meeting. You have the conversation.

Not because everything is perfect. Because you're still here. Still trying. Still showing up.
