Brand Collab Concept

Inside Out.

Liquid I.V. × Laneige

You replenish your body. You forget your skin.

Inside Out puts them there.

The Concept

Liquid I.V. owns the inside. Laneige owns the outside. Both brands are already talking to the same person, they just haven't been in the same room yet.

This isn't about aesthetics matching. It's about a gap that actually exists: the moment your body is being replenished and your skin is being forgotten. Neither brand has closed that gap alone. Together, they can.

Laneige Canada and Liquid I.V. Canada co-posted a giveaway: a product bundle which got 6,556 comments. There was value creation, but value was not further captured. There was no follow through. Inside Out is the campaign that should have come next.

My Take

Halftime. Everyone on the field sipping electrolytes, doing everything right. My face was itching, going red, breaking out from the sweat. I was holding a drink designed to fix exactly what was happening on the inside. Nothing existed for what was happening on the outside. The gap felt so obvious I couldn't believe nobody had closed it. This isn't a campaign I thought would look good in a portfolio. It's a campaign I genuinely wished existed. Those are the briefs worth writing.

The Campaign

A four-part system built around movement: before, during, after, and overnight. Liquid I.V. gives back what sweat takes. Laneige supports what sweat does to the skin. One routine. Two brands. Nothing left out.
